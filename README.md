# 🚀 Krestal AI

**Krestal AI** is an all-in-one, intelligent study platform engineered to help students eliminate weak spots, streamline daily tasks, and maintain study momentum through AI-driven analytics and empathetic guidance.

---

## ✨ Core Features & Modules

### 🛡️ Core Diagnostic & Academic Tools
* **Mistake Vault (For every type of student,with every curriculum):** Diagnostic error logging that analyzes root causes, classifies conceptual traps, and explain the markimg criteria of every curriculum in great depth and generates targeted re-quizzes to reclaim missed marks.
* **Lecture Recorder & Note Summarizer:** Real-time speech-to-text audio recorder that transcribes live classroom lectures, automatically logs quiz mistakes directly to the Mistake Vault, and turns raw notes into structured study concepts.
* **Adaptive Progress & Pacing Analytics:** Real-time syllabus and exam pacing tracker. Calculates required study speed leading up to exam dates and delivers targeted recommendations for weak topics.
* **Flashcards & Active Recall Studio:** High-yield active recall tool with built-in spaced repetition scheduling and an instant **AI Deck Generator**.

### 📝 Productivity & Writing
* **Smart Task & Schedule Planner:** Decomposes intimidating assignments into actionable 20–50 minute micro-task execution sprints to eliminate task paralysis.
* **Mood-Attuned Focus Sprints:** Pomodoro timer calibrated to your current mood and energy levels, featuring built-in binaural focus sounds and XP rewards.
* **Writing Assistant & Essay Critic:** Elevates academic prose by evaluating thesis strength, refining rhetorical nuance, and offering scholarly vocabulary suggestions.

### 🌟 Mentorship & Mindset Support
* **Extracurricular Guidance & Project Strategy:** AI roadmap and portfolio builder designed to turn curiosity into high-impact passion projects and student initiatives.
* **Empathetic AI Mentor:** Attuned student mentor and academic advisor providing real-time advice, schedule planning, and tailored study strategies.
* **Motivation SOS & Mindset Anchor:** Daily curated study quotes, emotional intelligence anchors, guided reflection prompts, and fast relief for study burnout.
